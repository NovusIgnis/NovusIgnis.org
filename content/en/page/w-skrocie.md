# Novus Ignis in brief

If you only have a moment, this page is enough to know who we are and what you can expect from us.

## Who we are

The Novus Ignis Foundation from Poznań (KRS 0001269575), founded by Marcin Wołowski, is an entity without shareholders: no one here shares out profits, and for our services we want to charge only as much as it costs to maintain them. More: [Foundation Principles whitepaper, chapter 1](../whitepaper-zasady-fundacji.md#s1) and [manifesto, section 1.1](../manifest.md#s1-1).

## What we want to do

We want to co-create an alternative internet whose highest value is human dignity, together with the privacy and freedom that follow from it. Suveria is to be a private space for work with optional help from digital intelligence, protected by safeguards whose operation can be independently verified. Omniforum is to be a shared public space where anyone can speak and be heard. More: [manifesto, introduction](../manifest.md#top) and [Why Novus Ignis](dlaczego.md).

## Where we are today

- **Working:** the foundation is registered, and the manifesto and whitepapers are published. The foundation accepts donations by bank transfer and shows every payment in the [public register of contributions and expenses](fundacja-rejestr.md); the first one was the founding fund.
- **Project:** Suveria is a project, and Omniforum has a first prototype under construction.
- **Hypothesis:** we assume that shared servers paid for by actual use may deliver more digital intelligence for every dollar than closed services; we have not tested this yet (manifesto, section 2.4).
- **Vision:** autonomous management by digital intelligence and further pillars. The full launch of further pillars is a distant direction; before that, only small initiatives with a defined end are possible. Today the foundation is run by people.

More: [manifesto, introduction](../manifest.md#top) and [Foundation Principles whitepaper, chapter 1](../whitepaper-zasady-fundacji.md#s1).

## What we will not do

We will not change these principles without changing the manifesto:

- We do not train models on conversations without separate consent (conversations are not used to improve digital intelligence unless someone separately allows it).
- We do not create hidden access to private content.
- A contribution does not grant votes, privileges or promotion.
- No one loses the right to vote because of their views or because they criticize the foundation.

Full list: [manifesto, chapter 9](../manifest.md#s9).

## What we are looking for

At the start, we are especially looking for a few people with whom we will build and test the first versions of Suveria and Omniforum:

- people working in security, cryptography and infrastructure;
- programmers and interface designers;
- lawyers specializing in foundation law, data protection and digital services;
- donors ready to fund the public pilot budget, with no influence on decisions and no right to promotion.

More: [manifesto, chapter 9](../manifest.md#s9), [Join us](dolacz.md) and [Foundation Principles whitepaper, section 8.3](../whitepaper-zasady-fundacji.md#s8-3).
